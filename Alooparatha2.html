Procedure

  1. Prepare the dough:

    • In a large bowl, combine the flour, salt, and oil.
    • Gradually add water, kneading until a soft and pliable dough forms.
    • Cover the dough with a damp cloth and let it rest for 30 minutes.

  2. Make the filling:

    • In a separate bowl, combine the mashed potatoes, cumin powder, coriander powder, red chili powder, garam masala, and salt.
    • Mix well until the ingredients are evenly distributed.

  3. Assemble the porota:

    • Divide the dough into equal-sized balls.
    • Roll out one ball into a thin circle.
    • Place a spoonful of the potato filling in the center of the circle.
    • Bring the edges of the dough together and seal tightly.
    • Roll out the sealed dough into a circle again.

  4. Cook the porota:

    • Heat a griddle or skillet over medium heat.
    • Place the porota on the griddle and cook until golden brown on both sides, cooking with a little oil or ghee.
    • You may need to press down on the porota with a spatula to cook evenly.
